Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026. A clear accounting of our data minimization protocols and operational boundaries.

Chitro is designed around anonymous identities, but anonymous does not necessarily mean that no technical information is processed. Certain information may be required to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the service.

1. Information We May Collect

Chitro adheres strictly to data minimization principles. We only collect and process data categories that are technically required to deliver our core communications functionality:

  • Anonymous account/identity information: System-generated anonymous user identifier keys managed via Firebase to maintain app sessions and recognize saved contacts without storing personal real-world identities.
  • Friend/connection information: Pointers linking anonymous identifier keys in Firebase to support user-authorized persistent friend relationships.
  • Subscription information: Purchase tokens and subscription entitlement validity provided via Google Play Store billing APIs. We do not process or store credit card or payment card details.
  • Device & app information: Device model, operating system version, and application build metadata required for client optimization and troubleshooting.
  • Technical & log information: Operational diagnostic logs and network transport records generated during client-server sync.
  • Notification information: Platform-issued push tokens where users voluntarily opt into notifications for incoming messages or friend pings.
  • Media information: Processed through Cloudinary only if media sharing features are explicitly utilized.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Chitro is exclusively allocated toward explicit operational obligations:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Chitro application.
  • To route real-time messages between participants using Ably.
  • To execute random partner search queues and pair available peers.
  • To store, manage, and render user-authorized friend rosters.
  • To validate and grant feature entitlement associated with active Play Store subscriptions.
  • To maintain infrastructure stability, investigate crash reports, and prevent automated abuse or rate abuse.
  • To enforce our Community Guidelines and safeguard users from malicious behavior.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Core infrastructure elements are delegated to established technology partners under strict service agreements:

Service Role Designated Provider Data Processed
Database & Storage Firebase Anonymous ID keys, connection links, and app metadata
Real-Time Messaging Ably Active message payloads, session tokens, and peer delivery packets
Media Storage Cloudinary Transmitted attachments or media tokens (when media sharing is enabled)
Payment Processing Google Play Store Transaction validation tokens, entitlement status, and platform receipts

4. Data Retention

We do not retain information longer than required for operational integrity and service stability:

  • Chat Messages: Transient by default; messages are processed in real-time and retained only for temporary transit and short-term delivery verification before being purged.
  • Account & Identity Metadata: Maintained for the duration of the active anonymous account instance. Once an account is deleted by the user, identity tokens are permanently deleted from Firebase.
  • Network & Operational Logs: Retained for a rolling period of up to 30 days for telemetry, abuse mitigation, and server security analysis before automatic deletion.

5. Account & Anonymous ID Deletion

When you delete your anonymous Chitro identity, the identity cannot be recovered. Deletion permanently removes or disconnects information associated with that identity from our Firebase databases, subject only to transient technical backup cycles.

Any subscription associated with the deleted anonymous identity will end and is not transferable to another anonymous identity.

6. Children's Privacy

Chitro is strictly intended for individuals who satisfy the age of consent. The minimum required age is 13 years old. Individuals under the age of 13 are strictly prohibited from using Chitro under any circumstances. If we determine that an account is operated by an individual under 13, the identity and its data are immediately terminated.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational precautions, including encrypted TLS in-transit communications and restricted database access control. However, no internet transmission or digital storage infrastructure is entirely infallible. We explicitly do not make claims of "100% security" or "guaranteed complete anonymity" against device-level vulnerabilities, local operating system compromises, or unauthorized client tampering.

Tier Architecture

Subscriptions

Clear, direct feature upgrades tied directly to your anonymous client identifier.

In-App Subscription Management

Chitro offers premium upgrades processed through Google Play. All tier capabilities, matching priorities, and subscription statuses are linked directly to your active anonymous device instance.

Active subscriptions are subject to Google Play billing regulations. You can cancel your subscription at any time via the Subscriptions section within your Google Play Store app settings.

Critical Subscription Rule: Subscription access is associated with the anonymous identity used to purchase or activate the subscription. Because anonymous identities cannot be recovered, deleting an anonymous identity permanently removes access associated with that identity. Subscriptions cannot be transferred to another anonymous identity, and purchases are non-refundable.

Safety Measures

Safety & Community Guidelines

Rules ensuring peer dialogues remain protective of privacy and free from harmful conduct.

Be Respectful

Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, extortion, or targeted degradation of another participant.

Protect Sensitive Data

Do not share passwords, financial records, home addresses, government IDs, or phone numbers with anonymous connections.

No Illegal Use

Chitro must never be utilized to promote or coordinate illegal acts, trade restricted contraband, or spread exploitative materials.

No Spam or Abuse

Automated scripts, repetitive unsolicited marketing, link injection, and service disruption activities are strictly prohibited.

Report Problems

Flag non-compliant behavior directly inside the chat interface to submit an incident review request.

Block Users

Terminate interaction immediately using the block tool. Blocked peers cannot match with you or access your messaging thread.
