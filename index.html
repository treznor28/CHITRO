Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026. A clear accounting of our data minimization protocols and operational boundaries.

Chitro is designed around anonymous identities, but anonymous does not necessarily mean that no technical information is processed. Certain information may be required to operate, secure, maintain, store chat history, and deliver published reels.

1. Information We May Collect

Chitro adheres strictly to data minimization principles. We only collect and process data categories that are technically required to deliver our core communications and social discovery features:

  • Anonymous account/identity information: System-generated anonymous user identifier keys managed via Firebase to maintain app sessions and recognize saved contacts without storing personal real-world identities.
  • Chats and Communication Data: Text messages, conversation references, and multimedia records routed via Ably and securely stored via Firebase Realtime Database/Cloud Storage to maintain chat records between confirmed friends.
  • Photos & Reel Media: Photos, reels, and video clips voluntarily uploaded by users to chats or the public Reels feed. Uploaded media is processed and delivered via Cloud Storage and Cloudinary CDN.
  • Feed Interactions & Metrics: View counts, likes, and comment threads associated with published Reels, linked to anonymous user IDs.
  • Friend/connection information: Pointers linking anonymous identifier keys in Firebase to support user-authorized persistent friend relationships.
  • Subscription information: Purchase tokens and subscription entitlement validity provided via Google Play Store billing APIs. We do not process or store credit card or payment card details.
  • Device & technical information: Device model, operating system architecture, build versions, and operational diagnostic logs necessary for stability and rate-limiting.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Chitro is exclusively allocated toward explicit operational obligations:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Chitro application.
  • To route instant messages and media notifications using Ably.
  • To store chat histories, photo exchanges, and friend connections in Firebase.
  • To publish, host, and render user-generated reels and photos via Cloudinary.
  • To record live view counts, calculate likes, and manage user comment threads on reels.
  • To enable sharing of reel assets into direct chats or third-party applications.
  • To validate and verify feature entitlement associated with active Google Play subscriptions.
  • To maintain infrastructure stability, investigate crashes, and prevent automated abuse.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Core infrastructure elements are delegated to established technology partners under strict service agreements:

Service Role Designated Provider Data Processed
Database & Cloud Storage Firebase Anonymous ID keys, saved chat messages, user photos, friend links, and reel engagement records
Real-Time Messaging Engine Ably Active message payloads, live session channels, and peer delivery packets
Media & Video CDN Cloudinary Published reel clips, user images, media caching, and video streaming optimization
Payment Processing Google Play Store Transaction validation tokens, entitlement status, and platform receipts

4. Data Retention

We do not retain information longer than required for operational integrity and service stability:

  • Chats & Photos: Stored on Firebase for the duration of the conversations between users. Deleting a chat or account removes references to stored media payloads.
  • Reels & Comments: Retained until manually deleted by the publishing user or removed as part of account deletion.
  • Account & Identity Metadata: Maintained for the duration of the active anonymous account instance. Once an account is deleted by the user, identity tokens are permanently deleted from Firebase.
  • Network & Operational Logs: Retained for a rolling period of up to 30 days for telemetry, abuse mitigation, and server security analysis before automatic deletion.

5. Account & Anonymous ID Deletion

When you delete your anonymous Chitro identity, the identity cannot be recovered. Deletion permanently removes or disconnects information associated with that identity from our Firebase databases, subject only to transient technical backup cycles.

Any subscription associated with the deleted anonymous identity will end and is not transferable to another anonymous identity.

6. Children's Privacy

Chitro is strictly intended for individuals who satisfy the age of consent. The minimum required age is 13 years old. Individuals under the age of 13 are strictly prohibited from using Chitro under any circumstances. If we determine that an account is operated by an individual under 13, the identity and its data are immediately terminated.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational precautions, including encrypted TLS in-transit communications and restricted database access control. However, no internet transmission or digital storage infrastructure is entirely infallible. We explicitly do not make claims of "100% security" or "guaranteed complete anonymity" against device-level vulnerabilities, local operating system compromises, or unauthorized client tampering.

Subscriptions

Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct clarifications regarding identity mechanics, interactive reels, message storage, and subscriptions.

What is Chitro?
Chitro is an anonymous social chat and community discovery platform designed by TREZNOR. It enables spontaneous, private peer conversations, real-time photo sharing, and user-generated reels discovery without public personal profiles.
How does the Reels section work? Can I upload photos and videos?
Yes! You can publish your own photos and reels directly to the community feed. Other users can view your media, leave likes, join the comment threads, and view the total view count. You can also share any reel directly into individual chats or outward to other installed applications.
Does Chitro store my chats and photos?
Yes. Active conversations, friend connections, and shared photos are stored securely using Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Storage, with live real-time message streaming powered by Ably. This ensures you can access your chats and multimedia with friends.
Is Chitro anonymous?
Yes. Chitro does not link your conversations, photos, or published reels to a real name or public personal profile. However, anonymous does not mean zero data; technical tokens and system identifiers are processed through Firebase and Ably to route messages and secure the platform.
How does Random Partner Search work?
Random Partner Search places your active session into a live matching queue, pairing you directly with another available user searching at that exact moment.
Can I add people as friends?
Yes. During or after conversations, you can send a mutual friend connection request. Once accepted, both parties retain access to direct messaging and saved photo exchanges.
Can I recover my anonymous ID after deleting it?
No. Deleting your anonymous Chitro ID is permanent. Because our system is built to decouple your personal identity, no recovery tokens exist.
What happens to my subscription if I delete my anonymous ID?
Any subscription associated with a deleted anonymous ID ends immediately. Subscriptions cannot be transferred to newly created anonymous accounts, and purchases are non-refundable.
What information does Chitro collect?
Chitro processes anonymous tokens via Firebase, message transit payloads via Ably, media files and reel assets via Cloudinary/Firebase, Play Store subscription verification tokens, and standard diagnostic logs.
Can I report another user or inappropriate content?
Yes. You can report violations directly through the options menu inside any active chat session or on any published reel.
Can I block someone?
Yes. Blocking a user immediately ends the conversation and prevents that identity from matching with you again in the random search queue or contacting you directly.
How do subscriptions work?
Subscriptions are managed directly through Google Play Store billing and are bound exclusively to your active anonymous device identifier.
How can I contact Chitro?
You can reach our administrative, legal, and support desks directly via email at treznornx1@gmail.com.
